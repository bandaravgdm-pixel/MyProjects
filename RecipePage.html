Guacamole

Welcome to the recipe of making guacamole.Fresh guacamole balances rich, buttery fat with sharp citrus acidity, savory aromatics, and a gentle kick of heat.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe Hass avocados, halved and pitted
  • 1 medium lime, juiced (about 2 tbsp)
  • 1/2 cup finely diced white onion
  • 1–2 fresh jalapeño or serrano peppers, seeded and minced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 medium Roma tomato, seeded and diced
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 bag sturdy salted tortilla chips

Instructions

  1. In a bowl or mortar, crush half of the diced onion, minced chili, and 1/4 teaspoon of salt with the back of a fork or pestle to draw out the natural juices and oils.
  2. Scoop the avocado flesh into the bowl. Press down with a fork or potato masher, intentionally leaving distinct pea-sized chunks rather than creating a smooth puree.
  3. Pour in the lime juice and gently fold in the remaining onion, minced chili, cilantro, and diced tomato with a spatula.
  4. Taste and adjust the salt. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to keep air out if storing temporarily.
